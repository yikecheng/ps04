# Brief-development instructions

Create exactly three briefs with piece IDs `01`, `02`, and `03`. Use only accepted exact evidence versions assigned to the relevant piece.

Each brief must:

- answer its assigned editorial question with one contestable central argument;
- contain explicit, atomic claims typed as `reported_fact`, `source_claim`, or `inference`;
- attach every claim to an exact written quote or existing transcript segment;
- use at least three independent publishers;
- include at least one inference connecting evidence from multiple publishers and explain the reasoning in `synthesis`;
- explain commercial or methodological incentives when relevant;
- state a meaningful limitation or counterargument; and
- avoid turning missing evidence into a confident conclusion.

The pieces must build on one another without repeating the same thesis: first establish what changed, then scrutinize claims and tensions, then derive bounded actions and watch signals for small teams.
