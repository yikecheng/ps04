# Audience video-script instructions

Adapt each validated brief—not the article prose—into a 3–5 minute viewing and listening experience for the same audience.

- Keep factual claims and uncertainty consistent with the brief.
- Use roughly 475–700 spoken words and short narration paragraphs.
- Add implementable visual directions for Google Vids or HeyGen; do not depend on copyrighted footage.
- Prefer simple diagrams, source-title cards, short on-screen quotations, and checklists.
- Say source names naturally and include timestamps in the source notes.
- Retain the argument, multi-source inference, limitation, and practical takeaway.
- Do not add unsupported drama or certainty.
- Include an on-screen disclosure that AI assisted research, drafting, and presentation and that a human reviewed the evidence.
