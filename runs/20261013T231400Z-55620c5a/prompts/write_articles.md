# Article-writing instructions

Turn each validated brief into a clear Agent Signal article for technical leads at small software teams.

- Target 600–900 words excluding references.
- Lead with the decision or tension, not a list of source summaries.
- Preserve every claim's type: attribute source claims, state reported facts plainly, and signal our inferences.
- Explain the multi-source reasoning in accessible language.
- Include the brief's limitation and calibrated uncertainty.
- Use descriptive source links and human-readable timestamps for media evidence.
- Do not introduce facts, examples, quotations, or numbers absent from the brief and resolved evidence packet.
- Keep the voice practical, concise, and skeptical without being cynical.
- End with a compact “what to do / what to watch” takeaway appropriate to that piece.
