# Selection-stage instructions

Apply the snapshotted editorial policy to every eligible evidence version. Return exactly one `accept`, `reject`, or `defer` decision per item.

For each reason:

- name the particular claims or observations that are usable or problematic;
- discuss audience relevance, novelty, source incentive, and evidentiary support;
- identify duplication or dependence on another underlying announcement;
- state uncertainty rather than using publisher reputation as a shortcut; and
- assign accepted evidence only to pieces where it advances the stated editorial question.

Favor a balanced evidence portfolio over a large pile of similar announcements. Preserve useful disagreements. A rejection is an editorial outcome, not a claim that the source is globally untrustworthy. A deferral must identify what is missing or what future evidence would change the decision.
